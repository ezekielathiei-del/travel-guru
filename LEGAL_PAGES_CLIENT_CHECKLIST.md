# Legal pages: client information needed before launch

The three legal pages are working drafts, not final legal advice. Do not publish them as final until the client confirms the details below and a qualified lawyer reviews them for the correct jurisdiction.

## Ask the client to confirm
- Full registered legal business name, registration number and physical/postal address.
- Country/countries where the business is registered and operates; governing law and relevant courts or dispute-resolution process.
- Official customer service, privacy and cancellation email addresses and telephone/WhatsApp number.
- Whether Travel Guru is acting as a travel agent, tour operator, package organiser, reseller or principal, and which suppliers it uses.
- Accepted payment methods and currencies; deposit amount, balance deadlines, payment fees and quotation validity.
- Supplier cancellation/refund rules, any Travel Guru cancellation or amendment fee, refund timelines, and how supplier cancellations are handled.
- What personal information is collected, who can access it, retention periods, any marketing practices and any cross-border transfers.
- Whether analytics, advertising pixels, embedded maps, chat tools or other cookies/tracking technologies are used.
- Any travel licences, insurance, bonding, consumer-protection or tourism disclosures required by local law.
