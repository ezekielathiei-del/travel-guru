WELCOME TO TRAVEL GURU

Travel is more than a destination.

It is about discovering the world, experiencing different cultures, creating lasting memories, and living life to the fullest.

Travel Guru is a travel and tour company dedicated to creating memorable, enriching, and seamless travel experiences for individuals, families, groups, and organisations.

We curate unique experiences that inspire people to explore more and make travel a meaningful part of their lifestyle. From exciting getaways and unforgettable safaris to cultural experiences, adventure travel, beach holidays, and tailor-made journeys, we connect our clients to experiences that leave a lasting impression.

Our goal is to take the stress out of travel while adding the fun, discovery, comfort, and excitement that make every journey worth remembering.

NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Can I customise a package?

Yes. Use Build Your Trip or send us your destination, dates, experiences and budget.

Can Travel Guru arrange flights and hotels?

These can be included in a proposal where available, with inclusions clearly shown.

Can I travel as a group?

Yes. We can prepare trips for couples, families, friends and corporate groups.

Can you arrange transfers and activities?

Yes. Add your requirements to the booking or custom trip form.

How do payments work?

Payment options will be added once the preferred payment provider is confirmed.

Can I request another destination?

Yes. Choose Other or I'm open to ideas and tell us what you have in mind.
