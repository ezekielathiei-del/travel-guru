Cancellation & Refund Policy

Understand how cancellation requests, supplier charges and refunds are handled before confirming a travel arrangement.

Draft for client review: This page contains working policy language and highlighted items that Travel Guru must confirm before publication. It is not a substitute for review by a qualified lawyer in the country where the business operates.

Effective date: [Insert publication date]

1. Important: check the booking confirmation

Cancellation and refund conditions can differ by airline, hotel, tour operator, activity and fare type. Before paying, read the specific quotation and booking confirmation carefully. Any supplier-specific cancellation conditions and deadlines should be shown to you before you commit.

2. How to cancel

Send a cancellation request in writing to [Insert official cancellation email or contact channel]. Include your full name, booking reference and the services you wish to cancel. A cancellation is effective when Travel Guru acknowledges receipt in writing. If departure is near, contact us by telephone as well.

3. Refund assessment

After receiving your request, we will review the applicable supplier rules, services already provided, amounts paid and any non-refundable charges. Where a refund is due, the amount may be reduced by permitted supplier cancellation fees, non-refundable deposits, ticket restrictions, payment processing costs where lawful and any other charges clearly disclosed in the booking terms. We will provide an explanation of the calculation.

4. Non-refundable services

Some flights, promotional fares, accommodation rates, permits, event tickets and third-party activities may be non-refundable or may carry substantial cancellation charges. We will communicate known restrictions before confirmation. Mandatory rights under applicable law are not affected.

5. Changes instead of cancellation

If you want to change your travel dates or arrangements, contact us as soon as possible. Changes depend on supplier approval and availability and may involve fare differences or amendment fees. We will confirm any additional cost before proceeding.

6. Cancellation by Travel Guru or a supplier

If Travel Guru or a supplier cancels a confirmed service, we will inform you and explain the available options under the booking terms and applicable law. Depending on the circumstances, options may include an alternative arrangement, a credit or a refund. We will not describe a credit as a cash refund.

7. Delays, disruptions and events outside reasonable control

Severe weather, natural disasters, public health events, government restrictions, security incidents, strikes and transport disruptions may affect travel. The options available depend on the supplier terms, the facts and applicable law. Contact us promptly if your journey is affected. Travellers are encouraged to consider suitable travel insurance.

8. Refund processing

Where a refund is approved, it will generally be returned to the original payment method where possible. The time required can depend on the supplier, bank and payment provider. [Client to confirm the normal processing target and whether any lawful administration fees apply.] We will update you if a third party is delaying the refund.

9. Travel insurance

We strongly recommend that travellers consider comprehensive travel insurance covering cancellation, medical expenses, disruption, baggage and other relevant risks. Insurance does not replace the need to understand the cancellation conditions for each service.

10. Contact us

For cancellation requests or questions, contact [Insert official email, telephone/WhatsApp and business address]. Please quote your booking reference.

11. Applicable law
